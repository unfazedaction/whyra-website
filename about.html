About WHYRA

Sailboat racing, sportsmanship, and a shared love of being on the water in Wilson Harbor, New York.

Wilson Harbor Yacht Racing Association

WHYRA brings together sailors who share an interest in racing on Lake Ontario. The association's website is a central place for racing schedules, published race results, and information about the sailing season.

Whether you race, crew, or follow the fleet, you can find the current schedule and published results here.

Our Racing Community
